Dapeng Shi

Postdoctoral Fellow
Department of Statistics and Data Science
The Chinese University of Hong Kong

I work with Prof. Junhui Wang at The Chinese University of Hong Kong. I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from the Shanghai Center for Mathematical Sciences, Fudan University, advised by Prof. Zhiliang Ying.

My research interests include statistical network analysis, graphical models, generative models, causal inference, and statistical genetics. My current work focuses on community detection, multilayer networks, and transfer learning for network data.
